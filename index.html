What Canopy Does

Superpowers for parents

Think of Canopy as a gentle guardian — always watching, never intrusive. Here's what it gives your family.

Activity Monitoring

See which apps and websites your children use. Clear, friendly reports — not surveillance, just awareness.

Screen Time Balance

Set healthy boundaries. Homework first, then play, then wind-down. Schedules that flex for weekends.

Content Filtering

Block harmful content before it arrives. Age-appropriate filters — and kids can always ask for access.

Location Awareness

Know where your children are without constant check-ins. Gentle alerts for school, home, and more.

Smart Alerts

"Emma wants more time on art class" — warm nudges, not alarming warnings. You decide what happens next.

Schedule Builder

Bedtime mode, homework time, free time — each with its own rules. Custom schedules for every child.
