Features

Everything growing families need

Canopy gives you gentle oversight without being intrusive. Know what matters, guide when needed, and let your family thrive.

Activity Monitoring

See which apps and websites your children use most. Understand their digital world with clear, friendly reports — not surveillance, just awareness.

Screen Time Balance

Set healthy boundaries for daily screen time. Create schedules that work for your family — homework first, then play, then wind-down.

Content Filtering

Block harmful content before it reaches your child. Age-appropriate filters that protect without over-restricting — they can always ask for access.

Location Awareness

Know where your children are without constant check-ins. Get gentle alerts when they arrive at or leave familiar places like school or home.

Smart Alerts

Receive warm, context-aware notifications. "Emma wants more time on art class" — not alarming warnings, just helpful nudges to guide decisions.

Schedule Builder

Create custom daily schedules for each child. Bedtime mode, homework time, free time — each with its own rules. Flexible for weekends and holidays.

How It Works

Three steps to peace of mind

Setting up Canopy takes less than five minutes. Here's how your family gets started.

1

Create your family

Sign up for a free account and add each family member. Give everyone a name, a profile color, and a role — parent or child.

2

Install on devices

Add the lightweight Canopy agent to your children's Mac. It runs quietly in the background — no slowdowns, no pop-ups, just protection.

3

Guide from anywhere

Open the Canopy app on your phone to see activity, adjust schedules, approve requests, and enjoy the calm of knowing your family is safe.
